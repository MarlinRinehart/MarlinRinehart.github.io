Who is Marlin Rinehart

Marlin Rinehart is a 25 year old Indie Game developer and inspiring Digital Cinematographer. Marlin Rinehart graduated Full Sail University with a Bachelors of Science in Game Design with a 3.41 GPA and is currently weighing on the decision to go back to college for a degree in Film.

Rinehart has always been a fan of games since he was 5 watching his brothers play the orginal Legends of Zelda: Ocarina of time on thier Nintendo 64. Since that point Marlin has strove to reignite the passion and creativity that games of that era could bring future generations. Marlin also has a passion in all things creative, from drawing (albeit badly), to coding small projects like this very website.
